The numbers in square brackets are the registry numbers of the papers and are the same in every volume. Each entry gives the authors, year, title, venue and DOI or URL, the stages it serves, the reading-list priority and identifier, the number of appended pages, the number of anchored passages, the pages from which it is cited, and a note when the appended file is not the typeset article (author manuscript, preprint, appendix, web capture or OCR copy).
