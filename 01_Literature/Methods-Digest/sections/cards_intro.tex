\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the cards}
\markboth{About the cards}{About the cards}
Each card summarises one paper's methodology in the same order: the stages it serves, then the aim, design, procedure, reliability, findings and the authors' limits, then \emph{Limits (card)} (the compiler's critique) and \emph{Use for this thesis}. Cards are grouped by the category of the paper. Each heading links to the reference and to the full text; every bracketed citation inside a card jumps to the exact sentence in the appended copy. Instrument appendices (rating forms, coding schemes) are folded into the card of their parent paper under their own number. Parker et al.\ (no.\,41) has no card because no full text could be obtained.
