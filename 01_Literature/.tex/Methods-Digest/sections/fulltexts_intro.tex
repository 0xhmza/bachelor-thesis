\noindent The pages that follow are the papers themselves, appended in registry order. A small tag in the page margin shows the paper number, the page within the paper, and links back to the card and the reference list. Cited sentences are highlighted in yellow; the orange mark beside a highlight links back to the sentence that cites it. Scanned papers carry an invisible OCR text layer so that the highlights can be placed. The bookmarks give each paper its section headings where they could be detected.
