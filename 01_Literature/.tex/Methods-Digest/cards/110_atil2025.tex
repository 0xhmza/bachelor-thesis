\begin{papercard}{atil2025}
\cf{Stages} \stagetag{L} (repeatability of LLM output under ``deterministic'' settings), \stagetag{I} (agreement of a model with itself across runs). arXiv preprint, version 5 of April 2025 (Penn State, Comcast and others). Added from the gap search of 23 October 2026 to support the methodology's three runs per unit.

\cf{Design} Five models set to be deterministic (temperature 0), eight tasks from two common benchmarks, ten runs each, zero-shot and few-shot \pc{atil2025}{1}{We investigate non-determinism in five LLMs configured to be deterministic when applied to eight common tasks in across 10 runs}.

\cf{Findings} Accuracy varied by up to 15\,\% between ordinary runs, and the gap between best and worst possible result reached 70\,\% \pc{atil2025}{1}{We see accuracy variations up to 15\% across naturally occurring runs with a gap of best possible performance to worst possible performance up to 70\%}; no model was repeatable across all tasks \pc{atil2025}{1}{none of the LLMs consistently delivers repeatable accuracy across all tasks, much less identical output strings}. The run distributions were not normal, so the authors avoid standard deviations of means \pc{atil2025}{4}{we do not report standard deviations of means because the distributions across runs for a given condition are not normally distributed}. Two metrics are proposed: agreement of raw output and of parsed answers over $N$ runs \pc{atil2025}{1}{TARr@N for the total agreement rate at N runs over raw output, and TARa@N for total agreement rate of parsed-out answers}.

\cf{Limits (card)} Hosted models of 2024 and multiple-choice benchmarks; non-determinism of a locally run open model with sampling disabled may be much lower (Xie et al.\ \pp{xie2025} chose that setting for this reason). Variation in free-text span extraction is not measured.

\cf{Use for this thesis} (1) Grounds for running each unit several times even at temperature 0 and for reporting the agreement of parsed labels across runs (TARa) as a stability figure, distinct from validity (Gilardi et al.\ \pp{gilardi2023}). (2) Supports the rule that a label returned in only one run is reviewed, not discarded. (3) Report the run-to-run range, not a mean $\pm$ SD, given non-normal distributions. (4) Record the model snapshot and serving setup with every run; Rodriguez et al.\ \pp{rodriguez2024} measured 89.5\,\% consistency for a hosted model.
\end{papercard}
