\section{Sample}
\label{sec:results-sample}
\paragraph{Search.} The search results and the store pages were collected on
21 November 2026 between 00:05 and 00:22~UTC. The 49 queries found 20 of the
25 well-known apps; the five missing apps were all companion apps of devices.
Of 66 generic terms that were tried, five were kept
(Table~\ref{tab:keywords}): \emph{fitness tracker} and \emph{Fitnesstracker}
found Garmin Connect and Zepp, \emph{smartband} found Zepp Life, and
\emph{Waage} (scale) found Withings, with \emph{scale} as its English pair. No
generic term found Polar Flow. With its 10{,}314 German reviews it would have
been selected, so the search misses at least this companion app of sports
watches. The 54 queries returned 1{,}220 results, between 8 and 30 per query.
They contained 704 different apps, and every store page could be read.

\paragraph{Selection.} Walking down the ranking, 53 apps were screened before
49 eligible apps were found. Four were excluded. Temple Run~2, a casino game
and a guitar tuner came in through words with a second meaning
(\emph{Laufen}, \emph{Glück} and \emph{Stimmung}) and are games or
general-purpose apps (X2). The meditation app Calm records only meditation
time (X4). The selected apps have between 4{,}142 and 99{,}995 German reviews.
In September 2026, Samsung devices produced 39.3\,\% of German mobile page
views, which is 54\,\% of the Android share, ahead of Xiaomi with
30\,\%~\parencite{statcounter2026os,statcounter2026vendor}. Its preinstalled
tracker, Samsung Health, meets the eligibility rules. With 33{,}757 German
reviews it would also have been selected by popularity.

\paragraph{The sample.} Appendix~\ref{app:sample} lists the 50 apps. Of the
25 well-known apps, 22 are in the sample; Natural Cycles and Sleep Cycle were
found but have fewer reviews than the last selected app. All 50 apps are free
to install, and 43 of the 49 ranked apps appear among the first ten results of
at least one query. By main function, 22 apps track activity, 9 are
multipurpose apps (eight of them companion apps of wearables or scales), 8
track diet and weight and 6 reproductive health (four cycle and two pregnancy
apps); two track mood, two sleep and one heart rate. The 50 apps come from 40
developers: Leap Fitness Group has eight apps in the sample, Simple Design
three and Google two.
