\section{Vendor Shares and Sensitivity Variants}
A vendor's share of its platform is its share of all mobile page
views in Germany~\parencite{statcounter2026vendor} divided by the platform's share of all mobile
page views~\parencite{statcounter2026os}, so that each vendor is compared within its own
platform.

Table~\ref{tab:sensitivity-variants} lists the variants of
the selection; each changes one rule of the main selection.
