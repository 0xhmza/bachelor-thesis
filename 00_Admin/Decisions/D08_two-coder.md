# D8 — How do two coders work?

- **Status:** proposed (2026-11-01). **Who the second coder is remains open and needs the supervisor.**
- **Affects:** RQ1–RQ4 · Methodology, Validation
- **Options considered:**
  1. One coder with team discussion, as Malki et al. 2024, p. 4 (no inter-rater reliability). Does not meet the meeting's request for two coders.
  2. Two coders on everything. Not feasible for one bachelor's thesis at 50 apps.
  3. **The author codes everything; a second coder codes the validation set independently under the frozen codebook, and reviews applicability, minimisation and mismatch decisions (chosen).**
- **Evidence:** meeting 2026-10-13, §6 ("use two coders"); McDonald et al. 2019 on when reliability measures fit; Campbell et al. 2013, pp. 4–5, 12: unitization is a source of disagreement, and negotiated agreement can help when one knowledgeable coder is available; Krippendorff 2019.
- **Choice:** units are fixed by a script before coding, so coders disagree about labels and not about units. Coders work independently; agreement is computed before any discussion; disagreements are then settled and recorded. If no second coder is available, the existing fallback applies: the supervisor reviews a sample, including judged non-mismatches.
- **Would change if:** the supervisor names a second coder or asks for a larger overlap.
