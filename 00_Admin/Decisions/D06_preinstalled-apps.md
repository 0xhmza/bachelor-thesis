# D6 — Which preinstalled (native) health apps enter the sample?

- **Status:** proposed (author's choice 2026-10-22; awaiting supervisor). The methodology states this choice.
- **Affects:** Methodology, App Sampling; walkthroughs (Apple Health needs an iPhone); [D1](D01_sample-size.md)
- **Options considered:**
  1. Exclude preinstalled apps and report the gap.
  2. Let them compete in the store ranking. Rejected: they reach users without a download, so rating count understates their exposure (Apple Health: 2,134 German ratings, against hundreds of thousands for leading downloaded trackers).
  3. **Reserve places for the preinstalled health or fitness trackers of the leading phone vendor of each platform (proposed, 25 October 2026).** Vendor share of a platform = its share of all German mobile page views ÷ the platform's share (StatCounter OS shares: Android 72.3 %, iOS 27.7 %, read 25 October).
  4. Vendors with at least 10 % of German mobile page views (first proposal, 22 October). Replaced: the 10 % has no source, is a parameter the result depends on, and ignores that the two platforms are treated equally everywhere else (D2).
  5. A fixed list (Apple, Samsung, Huawei, Google). Rejected: no stated reason for the list.
- **Evidence:**
  - Supervisor meeting of 13 October asked how Apple Health, Samsung Health and Huawei Health could be included ([organized notes](../Meeting-Notes/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Organized.md)).
  - German mobile vendor shares, September 2026, by mobile page views: Samsung 39.34 %, Apple 27.63 %, Xiaomi 21.4 %, unknown 3.96 %, Huawei 1.54 %, Google 1.46 % ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/vendor-market-share/mobile/germany), checked 2026-10-22). Page views are a proxy for the installed base, not sales.
  - Shares within the platform: iOS, Apple 100 %; Android, Samsung 39.34 / 72.3 = 54 %, Xiaomi 21.4 / 72.3 = 30 %, Huawei 2 %, Google 2 %, unknown 5 %. The leading vendors are Apple and Samsung. Xiaomi would enter under a 10 %-of-all-page-views rule or a rule that covers 80 % of Android (Samsung + Xiaomi = 84 %), but contributes no app (below), so every variant gives the same three apps.
  - Preinstalled trackers of the vendors above 10 % (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi):
    - Apple: Apple Health and Apple Fitness, the latter available to all iPhone users without an Apple Watch since iOS 16. **Correction, 23 October 2026:** Apple Health does have an App Store listing (`com.apple.Health`, id 1242545199, released 25 October 2021, 2,134 German ratings; Apple lookup API, German storefront); an earlier version of this record said it had none. Samsung Health also has an App Store listing for iPhone (id 1224541484, 17,598 German ratings), which is the same product as the preinstalled Android app. (secondary coverage, e.g. [iPhone in Canada, 6 June 2022](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2022/06/06/ios-16-to-bring-the-fitness-app-to-all-iphones-no-apple-watch-needed/); the Apple newsroom release was not found). Apple Fitness also has an App Store listing (3,012 German ratings in the 20 October trial).
    - Samsung: Samsung Health, also listed in both stores (sampling report (removed on 25 October 2026; in Git history)).
    - Xiaomi: no preinstalled tracker on international software could be identified. "Mi Health" appeared as a system app in a Chinese MIUI beta ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/mi-health-app-miui-beta/amp)); no source confirms it on current global HyperOS phones.
- **Choice (proposed):** Apple Health, Apple Fitness and Samsung Health take three reserved places. Their store listings (Apple Health, Apple Fitness and Samsung Health on the App Store; Samsung Health on Google Play) are removed from the store rankings, and Apple and Samsung count as providers already in the sample, so their other apps are skipped by the developer rule of D2. The ranked selection continues to 47 (D1, D2). Huawei Health, Google's apps and Xiaomi's are not included by the rule.
- **Rule (25 October 2026, replaces the 10 % threshold):** per platform, the vendor with the largest share of that platform's German mobile page views; the number of vendors is therefore two by construction and no cut-off is tuned. Sensitivity, reported with the result: vendors above 10 % of all page views; vendors covering 80 % of the platform. Both give the same three apps.
- **Reason:** Preinstalled apps are present on most German phones without a choice by the user, which is the strongest form of exposure, yet they escape a download- or rating-based ranking. The leading vendor of a platform is the configuration a German user of that platform most often meets, which is the logic of selecting the most widely used apps, and per-platform shares keep the equal treatment of iOS and Android used in D2. Page views are a proxy for the installed base, not sales.
- **Would change if:** a preinstalled tracker on a leading vendor's phones is dropped or a new one is confirmed; the leading vendor of a platform changes; a preinstalled tracker on Xiaomi's international phones is confirmed and the supervisor prefers a coverage rule (it would add a place, giving 46 + 4); Health Connect (Android's system health store) is judged to be a tracker under the D3 definition.
