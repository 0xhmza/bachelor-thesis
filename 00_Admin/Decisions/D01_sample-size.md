# D1 — How many apps, and how are they split?

- **Status:** proposed (author's choice 2026-10-22; awaiting supervisor). The methodology states this choice.
- **Affects:** Methodology, App Sampling; workflow figure; abstract
- **Options considered:**
  1. Proposal: 50 apps with quotas, 30 fitness and 20 fertility.
  2. 50 apps without domain quotas, all from the store ranking.
  3. **50 apps without domain quotas: 47 from the store ranking plus 3 preinstalled apps (proposed).**
  4. 45 + 5 preinstalled apps.
- **Evidence:**
  - Supervisor meeting of 13 October: "no 30 health app, 20 fertility,.. just the first 50 apps according to the methodology" ([raw notes](../Meeting-Notes/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Raw.md)).
  - The same meeting asked how Apple Health, Samsung Health and Huawei Health could enter the sample ([organized notes](../Meeting-Notes/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Organized.md)). Preinstalled apps have no download step, so their rating counts understate their exposure (Apple Health: 2,134 German App Store ratings; an earlier version said it had no listing, corrected 23 October), and they need their own places.
  - The vendor rule in [D6](D06_preinstalled-apps.md) yields exactly three preinstalled apps. A fourth or fifth place would have to admit vendors that do not lead their platform (Xiaomi 30 % of Android, Huawei 2 %, Google 2 %), which the exposure argument does not support.
  - Comparable studies that analyse the documents *and* inspect the apps themselves worked with 20 to 64 apps: Malki et al. (2024) 20 apps, all installed and inspected (card `malki2024`, p. 4); Huckvale et al. (2019) 36 apps with traffic and policy analysis (card `huckvale2019`, p. 3); Hutton et al. (2018) 64 apps with a heuristic evaluation of interface, terms and policy (card `hutton2018`, p. 5). Document-only reviews go higher (O'Loughlin et al. 2019: 116 eligible apps). A target of 50 lies inside the range of the in-depth designs. Added 2026-10-25.
- **Choice (proposed):** a target of 50 apps; 3 places for the preinstalled apps from D6 and 47 for the store ranking in D2. All eligible health trackers compete in one ranking; categories describe the resulting sample and impose no quotas. The author confirmed this single study domain on 28 October 2026 ([D15](D15_health-tracker-domain.md)). If fewer than 47 eligible store apps are found, all are kept and the shortfall reported.
- **Reason:** The total of 50 comes from the proposal and the supervisor. The number of preinstalled places is set by a rule, not chosen to fill a round number, and the ranking fills the rest. Dropping quotas follows the supervisor's instruction.
- **Would change if:** the supervisor wants quotas back; D6 yields a different number of preinstalled apps; the eligible pool is smaller than 47.
