# D18 — What is the primary weighting unit of RQ1?

- **Status:** proposed (2026-11-04, submission review R8). Needs the author's and supervisor's confirmation.
- **Affects:** Methodology § Comparison and Reporting by Research Question; Results RQ1.
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Equal weight per app: proportions computed per app, then averaged (proposed).**
  2. Pooled over all applicable pathways (each pathway counts once).
  3. Equal weight per provider (differs only for Apple, with two apps).
- **Reason:** providers that document more data flows have more applicable pathways; pooling would let them dominate RQ1. Option 2 is reported as a secondary view.
- **Also:** the two uncertain-pathway calculations are sensitivity scenarios with stated numerators and denominators, not bounds.
- **Would change if:** the supervisor prefers pathway-level pooling as the primary view.
