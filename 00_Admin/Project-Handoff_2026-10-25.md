## 1. The thesis

*Understanding Privacy Harm Communication in Health Tracking Apps* (B.Sc. IT-Sicherheit,
RUB; advisor Kavous Salehzadeh Niksirat, MPI-SP; first examiner proposed: Carmela
Troncoso, unconfirmed; second TODO). Document analysis plus an adapted cognitive
walkthrough of 50 apps; LLM-assisted coding checked by blind manual coding.

- **RQ1 Coverage:** which data flows, capabilities and harms the communication mentions, and which applicable harms of the reference model it omits.
- **RQ2 Stance:** how named harms are acknowledged, qualified, minimised, denied or shifted to the user.
- **RQ3 Protection:** which measures and benefits are claimed, and how their scope and strength relate to the reference model.
- **RQ4 Surfaces:** how coverage, stance and protection differ between policies, listings, marketing and inspected interfaces.

Authority: `03_Thesis/thesis.tex` and its chapters; the 13 October supervisor
meeting ([organized](Meeting-Notes/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Organized.md),
[raw](Meeting-Notes/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Raw.md)) supersedes the proposal
(`03_Thesis/proposal/`, frozen 5 October snapshot).
