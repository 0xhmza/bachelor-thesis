\chapter{App Sample}
\label{app:app-sample}
The 50 apps of the sample (Section~\ref{sec:results-sample}). Numbers 1--47 are
the ranked store apps in the order of German exposure; 48--50 are the
preinstalled apps. \emph{Listed in} gives the stores in which the app was found.
\emph{Ratings} is the rating count of the German App Store (23 October 2026);
\emph{Reviews} is the count of written reviews on German Google Play (24 and 25
October 2026). A dash means that the app has no listing in that store.
Categories follow the domain of Section~\ref{sec:app-sampling}. The rows are
generated by \texttt{scripts/app-ranking/v003/rank.py}.
