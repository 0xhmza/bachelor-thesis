\chapter{App Sample}
\label{app:app-sample}
The 50 apps of the sample (Section~\ref{sec:results-sample}). Numbers 1--47 are
the ranked store apps in the order of selection, alternating between Google Play
and the App Store; 48--50 are the preinstalled apps. Ratings are the store's own
count on the day of collection: worldwide for Google Play (24 October 2026) and
for the German storefront for the App Store (23 October 2026), so the two columns
cannot be compared. Categories follow the domain of Section~\ref{sec:app-sampling}.
The rows are generated by \texttt{scripts/app-ranking/v002/rank.py}.
