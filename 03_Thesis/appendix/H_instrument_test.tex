\chapter{Instrument Test Records}
\label{app:instrument-test}

These are the complete records of the test of Section~\ref{sec:results-test}. They were prepared by an AI
assistant and are for the author to check. Policy units are located by ID and heading. The study's own wording
is used here; the exact evidence spans stay with the raw model output in
\texttt{05\_Data/raw/policy-test\_2026-11-01/}, together with the archived policy
(SHA-256 \texttt{09d9b479\ldots{}1321}).
