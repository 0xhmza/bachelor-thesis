\section{Timeline}
\label{sec:timeline}

A bachelor's thesis at RUB Informatik runs three months from registration with
the examination office, and registration should not happen before the proposal
is approved and the sampling is settled. The schedule below therefore has two
parts: a preparation phase that is already under way, and a twelve-week
execution phase keyed to the registration date. The dates in the second block
assume registration on Monday, 2~November~2026; if registration moves, the whole
block moves with it.

\paragraph{Where this plan can slip, and what absorbs it.}
Two dependencies are outside the author's control, and both are deliberately
front-loaded into the preparation phase: approval of this proposal, and the
written answer on whether an ethics review is needed. Neither blocks work that
can proceed in the meantime --- the literature review, the sampling protocol,
and the first codebook and prompt versions all continue regardless. Inside the
execution phase, the two tasks with genuinely uncertain duration are material
collection (policies move, links die) and the validation loop, which may require
a second codebook revision. The holiday weeks and the closing buffer are sized
to absorb one full revision cycle of the latter without moving the submission
date.
