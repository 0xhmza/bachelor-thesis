# D4 — How do price, subscriptions and accounts affect eligibility?

- **Status:** proposed (author's choice 2026-10-23; awaiting supervisor). Fixed before the 23 October collection.
- **Affects:** Methodology, App Sampling (eligibility E3) and App Walkthroughs; [sampling protocol](../../../04_Study/4.4_App-Sampling/sampling_protocol.md) v2
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Free to install for the sample; payment, trial and account requirements recorded and applied only to walkthrough eligibility (proposed).**
  2. Free to use: exclude apps whose tracking needs a subscription. Rejected for the sample: whether a feature is paywalled is often visible only after installation, and the document analysis needs no purchase.
- **Evidence:**
  - Robillard et al. (2019) and the proposal included apps that "can be downloaded for free"; Malki et al. (2024) did not exclude apps with in-app purchases.
  - Supervisor meeting of 13 October: distinguish free to install from free to use and decide how trials requiring a credit card are handled ([organized notes](../../Meetings/2026-10-13_Supervisor-Meeting_Organized.md) §4).
  - The study's ethics rules forbid payment and identity verification in walkthroughs ([ethics](../../../04_Study/4.8_Ethics/README.md)); the methodology already skips such apps there with a recorded reason.
- **Choice (proposed):** eligibility E3 is "free to install" (store price 0 in the German storefront). In-app purchases, subscriptions and card-required trials are recorded per app from the store listing; they do not exclude an app from the sample. They decide only walkthrough eligibility, where an app that cannot reach its tracking function without payment is skipped and reported.
- **Reason:** The document corpus (policy, store texts, provider pages) is reachable without payment, so a paywall does not limit what can be analysed for RQ1–RQ3. Users of freemium apps meet the same communication, so excluding them would remove widely used apps for a reason unrelated to the research questions.
- **Would change if:** the supervisor prefers free to use; many selected apps turn out to need payment before any tracking, which would leave too few walkthrough candidates.
