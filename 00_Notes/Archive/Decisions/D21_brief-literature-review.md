# D21 — The literature review is brief (at most ten papers), not a systematic review

- **Status:** confirmed by the author for application on 2026-11-06 (notes of the supervisor meeting, marked "apply that now"). The ten-paper selection was proposed by the AI assistant and confirmed by the author in chat the same day ("Keep the ten"); the orange marks are removed. Supersedes the method of [D13](D13_literature-review.md) and [D14](D14_literature-review-v2.md) for the thesis; the search run, its data and the records stay.
- **Affects:** Methodology 4.2 (rewritten), Methodology 4.3 (wording about the review evidence), workflow figure; Results 5.1 (rewritten); Appendix F (removed from `thesis.tex`); Appendix G (note on K1–K8); [`99_Archive/2026-11-06_systematic-literature-review/`](../../../99_Archive/2026-11-06_systematic-literature-review/README.md) (removed text); Related Work (to be restructured, see the meeting notes §6).
- **Options considered:**
  1. Keep D14: a scoping review in the sense of Arksey and O'Malley, reported with PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-S and PRISMA-ScR (term groups, four strands, eight venues, four databases, rules R1–R8, flow diagram). Rejected: the thesis is not a review paper.
  2. Keep the search but report it in an appendix only. Rejected: the methodology would still describe a protocol the study does not need, and Results 5.1 already carried a notice that it did not match the methodology.
  3. **A brief literature review of at most ten papers, chosen by reading, with two purposes: the relevant literature and the ground of the reference harm trees (chosen).**
- **Evidence:**
  - Supervisor meeting of 6 November ([raw notes](../../Meetings/2026-11-06_Supervisor-Meeting_Raw.md), [organized](../../Meetings/2026-11-06_Supervisor-Meeting_Organized.md) §3): PRISMA and Boolean expressions belong to review papers; the thesis is not one; a brief review section is enough, covering relevant literature and the ground for the reference harm tree "like Knabenhans et al. did"; at most ten papers. Method literature belongs in the section that uses the method.
  - Page et al. (2021), p. 178 (checked in the PDF): PRISMA 2020 is "a reporting guideline designed to address poor reporting of systematic reviews". The guideline serves a paper whose result is the claim that its search found the studies there are.
  - Knabenhans et al. (2026), §§4.2–4.3 (checked in the PDF): the harms for each use case are extracted "from existing literature" and expanded with the authors' own analysis; the trees are then built from them. The paper cites a handful of sources per use case, not a systematic corpus.
  - The pathways of the reference model v2 cite seven papers: Knabenhans et al. (9 of 21 pathways), Malki et al. (5), Niksirat et al. (4), Hudig and Singh (4), Cao et al. (3), Gabriele and Chiasson (2), Citron and Solove (2). Counted from `03_Thesis/tables/reference-pathways.tex`, generated from `reference_model_v2.yaml`. The tree therefore already rests on fewer than ten papers.
- **Choice:**
  - Methodology 4.2 states the purpose, three selection criteria (L1 domain, L2 content, L3 type), the preference for diversity when more than ten papers qualify, and the extraction (harms, capabilities, actor, data or component, page or section). No Boolean strings, strands, venue listing, deduplication rules, screening rules or flow diagram.
  - The ten (confirmed): Knabenhans et al. 2026; Citron and Solove 2022; De and Le Métayer 2016; Salehzadeh Niksirat et al. 2024; Gabriele and Chiasson 2020; Malki et al. 2024; Hudig and Singh 2025; Cao et al. 2024; Shipp and Blasco 2020; Huckvale et al. 2019. The first eight are the sources of the pathways plus the origin of harm trees; the last two are the relevant literature on policy and data-flow analyses.
  - Method literature (Hutton et al., Robillard et al., O'Loughlin et al., Zou et al., Baumann et al., Törnberg and others) stays in the methodology section that uses it and is not part of the ten.
  - Results 5.1 reports the ten papers and the number of pathways each supplies.
- **Consequences:**
  - D14's procedure, its rules C1–C4 and R1–R8, the verification of the AI screening (D14, 4 November) and the PRISMA reporting items no longer apply to the thesis. The record of the 28 October run remains in `scripts/literature-search/` and `05_Data/Literature Search/28. Oct/`.
  - The capability numbers K1–K8 of the reference model came from the capability table of that run. The numbers stay, because the codebook and the stored labels use them; Appendix G says where they come from. The reasoned claim that K1–K8 rest on a coded corpus of 51 studies is no longer made.
  - The gap statements of the archived Results 5.1 (LQ2: no study codes which harms communication names; LQ4: only one study analyses a health provider's own privacy text) are the evidence for the Related Work restructuring. They are not in the thesis now; the restructuring is an open point.
  - D12 states "if the review evidence … documents it": read as the papers of this review or another cited source.
- **Would change if:** the author names other papers; the pathways of the reference model cite a source outside the ten (add it or drop the pathway); the Related Work restructuring needs a wider literature base (that would be Related Work, not this review).
