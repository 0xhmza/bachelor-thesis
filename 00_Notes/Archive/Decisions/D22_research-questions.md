- **Choice (wording):** the research question is unchanged. The sub-questions are:
  - RQ1 Coverage: Which data flows, capabilities and harms do the privacy policy and the interface of a health tracking app mention, and which of the harms that apply to that app do they leave out?
  - RQ2 Stance: When the policy or the interface names a harm, how do they word it: do they acknowledge it, qualify it, minimise it, deny it or present it as the user's responsibility?
  - RQ3 Protection: Which protective measures and benefits do the policy and the interface claim, and how do the scope and strength of each claim relate to the pathways to harm that the measure would have to cut?
  - RQ4 Surfaces: How do coverage, stance and protection claims differ between the privacy policy and the interface of the same app, and can the information that the policy gives also be found in the interface?
  The wording adds no new measure: each question keeps the measure and method of the table (Axis A and the coverage level, Axis B, Axis C and the protection check, the same codes on both surfaces and the walkthrough).
