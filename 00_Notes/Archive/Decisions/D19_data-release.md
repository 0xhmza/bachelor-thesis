# D19 — Which study records are released, and on what terms?

- **Status:** proposed — decided by the AI assistant at the author's instruction of 2026-11-04 (night); marked orange in the thesis; confirm the release with the chair before submission. Not legal advice.
- **Affects:** Methodology § Data and Artifact Availability.
- **Options considered:**
  1. Release everything, including captured policies, interface transcripts and recordings.
  2. **Release the instruments, scripts and labels openly; keep the captured third-party texts as research copies for the examiners; keep recordings and screenshots private (chosen).**
  3. Release nothing beyond the thesis.
- **Evidence:** § 60d UrhG (copies for text and data mining for non-commercial scientific research; para. 4: making them available only to a defined group for joint research and to individual third parties for checking the quality of the research; checked at gesetze-im-internet.de, 2026-11-04). Recordings show advertisements, operating-system content and study accounts (walkthrough protocol v8).
- **Choice:** released openly: scripts, reference model, codebook, prompts, sampling manifest, model and human labels with unit IDs and span positions, decision records. Captured policy and interface texts: identified by address, capture date and SHA-256 hash; kept as research copies and made available to the examiners only. Screen recordings and screenshots: private.
- **Reason:** everything needed to check a label or an estimate is released, and every text can be traced to its archived capture, without republishing works of third parties.
- **Would change if:** the chair or a provider's terms allow more, or the examination office requires a different deposit.
