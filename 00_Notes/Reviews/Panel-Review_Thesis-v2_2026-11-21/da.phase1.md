## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). Read adversarially, this dimension asks whether the study design could survive a hostile replication attempt: whether every step from data collection to reported result is specified tightly enough that another researcher would reach the same numbers, whether data handling hides discretionary choices that could move the outcome, and whether any statistics or counts are reported with the denominators, rules and uncertainty needed to check them. I will not score it, but I will raise challenges where a methodological weakness becomes a logical weakness in the argument.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat). From a challenger's position, this dimension tests whether the manuscript's picture of the field is accurate or convenient: whether prior work is cited for what it actually found, whether contrary findings are omitted, whether terminology is used in its established sense, and whether factual statements about the domain would hold up against the cited sources. I will not score it, but cherry-picked or misrepresented literature that props up the core argument falls within my challenge remit.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the DA seat). This is my dimension. It asks whether the central thesis holds together as an argument: whether conclusions follow from the evidence actually presented rather than from assumed premises, whether claims made in one part of the manuscript are contradicted or quietly narrowed elsewhere, whether the reasoning contains circularity, false dichotomies, overgeneralization or unsupported leaps, and whether the strongest counter-argument is acknowledged and answered. A paper fails here when its stated conclusions would not survive a reader who accepts the evidence but refuses any unstated step.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat). Adversarially, this dimension asks whether a reader from a neighbouring field could follow and trust the framing, or whether key definitions, concepts and implications only make sense inside one community's assumptions, and whether any claim that crosses disciplinary lines is backed by evidence rather than assertion. I will not score it, but ignored stakeholder viewpoints and unexamined disciplinary framing can feed my challenges.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the EIC seat). From a challenger's view, this dimension asks whether the organisation, exposition, figures and tables make the argument easy to verify or instead make weaknesses hard to locate, and whether the manuscript follows the conventions expected of its genre. I will not score it; I will note structure only where it obscures a logical gap.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the EIC seat). Adversarially, this dimension asks whether the work is original and significant enough to matter to its intended readership, and whether it survives the "so what?" question: what changes in the field if the conclusions are true. No target venue has been confirmed for this call, so I make no venue-alignment claim; I will not score it, but a contribution claim that the argument does not support is a coherence challenge I may raise.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the core thesis and each load-bearing claim follow from the evidence actually presented; hidden or unstated premises; circular reasoning; overgeneralization beyond the scope the method can support; contradictions between aims, stated rules, results and conclusions across sections; and whether the strongest counter-argument and plausible alternative explanations are acknowledged and answered.
what_triggers_block: At least one load-bearing claim rests on an unsupported inferential leap, an internal contradiction or an unaddressed alternative explanation that breaks the argument as written, although added reasoning, evidence or a narrower stated scope could repair it within a revision.
what_triggers_warn: Peripheral claims overreach their evidence, or minor inconsistencies, unstated assumptions or unanswered obvious counter-arguments weaken the argument without breaking the chain from evidence to the central conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis is circular, self-contradictory or contradicted by the manuscript's own evidence, so that no revision short of rebuilding the central argument could make the main conclusions follow.
